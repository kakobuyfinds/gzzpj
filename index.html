The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated list of product links intended to help shoppers organize items they may want to purchase using Kakobuy. Useful lists include categories, product details and comparison notes. A listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

This page provides a category-first discovery guide linking to CAFANS; it is not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Kakobuy service.

A practical Kakobuy spreadsheet guide

Build a shortlist with a purpose.

Three checks that make product discovery more useful.

01 / FIND YOUR CATEGORY

Start with the wardrobe gap.

If you are browsing a Kakobuy shoes spreadsheet, decide whether you need an everyday sneaker or a more specific silhouette. For a Kakobuy clothing spreadsheet, begin with the layer you are missing: T-shirts, hoodies, sweaters, jackets, pants or shorts.

02 / READ THE DETAILS

Compare more than the image.

Review size charts, garment measurements, material details and seller information. Available quality-control (QC) photos may help you inspect visible details, but they cannot establish every aspect of quality or authenticity. Keep notes beside your Kakobuy product finds.

03 / CHECK THE FULL PICTURE

Budget before you commit.

Consider item price, delivery charges, service fees and any applicable taxes. When using a Kakobuy accessories spreadsheet, also check dimensions, metal composition and fastening details. Confirm current availability and return conditions before purchasing.

Keep this comparison checklist

What should you check before choosing an item?

Use the same questions across product links so your shortlist compares relevant details instead of relying on a title or a single image.

Category-specific checks for more informed product comparisons.
Product typeCompare firstUseful question
Shoes & sneakers Size chart, foot-length guidance, upper materials and sole details. Does the measurement describe foot length or insole length?
Hoodies, sweaters & jackets Chest, shoulder width, body length, sleeve length and fabric composition. How do the measurements compare with a layer I already wear?
T-shirts & jerseys Garment width, length, fabric weight where supplied and print details. Is the listed fit supported by actual garment measurements?
Pants, shorts & sets Waist range, rise, inseam and separate top-and-bottom measurements. Will both pieces fit, or am I judging the set by only one size label?
Headwear & accessories Circumference, adjustability, dimensions, materials and closures. Are the dimensions and material details clearly stated?
Underwear Waist measurements, fabric composition and item-specific return conditions. Have I checked the sizing and return restrictions before ordering?

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated list of product links intended to help shoppers organize items they may want to purchase using Kakobuy. Useful lists include categories, product details and comparison notes. A listing alone does not verify quality, authenticity or availability.

Is this an official Kakobuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent discovery guide with links to CAFANS categories. It is not an official Kakobuy website and does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, compare the listed size chart with your foot measurements and check whether measurements refer to foot length or insole length. For clothing, compare garment measurements with a similar item you own. Review material information and available quality-control photos.

What should I include in my shopping budget?

Consider the item price, domestic delivery, any service or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current charges with the relevant seller and service before paying.

Do the brand illustrations verify products or authenticity?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès visuals are illustrative artwork, not product photos or verified listings. They do not establish stock, brand affiliation, authorization or authenticity.
